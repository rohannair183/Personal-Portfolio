# What does clean look like?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

"Clean" is doing a lot of work. Pin it down with references and anti-references.

1. Name 2–3 sites you like the *feel* of (not necessarily personal sites). What specifically do you like: whitespace, typography, dark vs light, density, animation?
2. Name 1–2 sites or patterns you do *not* want (e.g. flashy dev portfolio, corporate SaaS marketing, brutalist, too minimal).
3. Photo of you on the home page: yes, no, or only on an about page?
4. Color: mostly monochrome, one accent, or comfortable with more?

## Comments

## Answer

**Primary reference:** [gwern.net](https://gwern.net/) — simple layout, topic-grouped index, text-first, no gimmicks.

**Secondary reference:** [stevengong.co](https://stevengong.co/) — Obsidian-backed notes, maps of content, "second brain" feel. Borrow the MOC / graph idea, not the emoji-heavy chrome or dark-mode toggle unless you want that later.

**Photo:** yes, on the home page.

**Color:** monochrome only. Black, white, grays. No accent color.

**Anti-references:** none named. Default steer: skip flashy dev portfolios (3D, heavy animation) and corporate SaaS marketing pages.
