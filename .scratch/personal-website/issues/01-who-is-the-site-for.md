# Who is the site for?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

Who is this site for, and what should they take away after one visit?

Pick the closest fit, or mix if honest:

1. **Future you** — a public notebook so you can find your own threads later
2. **Peers** — people who share your interests (ML, whatever you're into now) looking for signal, not a hire-me pitch
3. **Hiring managers** — professional presence, but interests and learning front and center instead of a classic portfolio
4. **Everyone above** — with a clear primary audience and the others secondary

Also: should the tone read more like a blog, a lab notebook, or a quiet personal site with occasional essays?

## Comments

## Answer

**Mix.** Primary audience: hiring managers. They should leave knowing you're sharp, curious, and actively learning, not just a list of job titles. Secondary audience: future you, using the site as a searchable public notebook.

Tone: professional but honest. Closer to a learning log than a marketing site. Show your thinking, including what you don't know yet. LinkedIn stays the formal credential layer; this site shows how you think.
