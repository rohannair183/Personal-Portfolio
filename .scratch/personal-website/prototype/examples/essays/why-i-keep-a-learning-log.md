---
title: Why I keep a learning log
date: 2026-08-15
summary: Public notes force me to explain things in plain language, which is the fastest way to find gaps.
tags: [meta, learning]
moc: meta
draft: false
---

Most of what I study doesn't stick unless I write it down. A learning log is lower stakes than an essay: I can be wrong, leave questions open, and come back later.

Hiring managers who read these get a better signal than a project list alone. They see how I think when something is new, not just the finished artifact.
